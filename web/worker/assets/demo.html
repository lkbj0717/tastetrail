    03 / Behind your recommendation
    From your ratings to a reasoned choice.
    Our final Agent + Qloo system connects personal history with cultural evidence.
    Select a step to explore ↙
    Step 01 / Your film history
    Same starting point
    We check the film titles, years, and ratings. All three methods receive the same eight ratings and two new candidates, so the comparison starts from the same information.
    Step 02 / Preference agent
    Understand both likes and dislikes
    The planner proposes tentative preferences, with supporting films, counterexamples, and unknowns. Numerical tools use patterns in other users’ ratings to estimate which candidate fits this history.
    Step 03 / Qloo evidence
    Culture adds another perspective
    Two groups of liked films and one group of disliked films query the same candidate pair. Qloo’s themes, tone, and cultural associations help test the preference hypotheses. Stronger association with dislikes can count against a candidate.
    Step 04 / Review & combine
    Check before choosing
    A reviewer weighs both candidates against the preference hypotheses and contrary history. A separate decision layer combines this review with the initial model estimate and numerical preference signals, including Qloo-informed taste groups.
    Step 05 / Film recommendation
    See the choice and its basis
    The result shows the selected film, its explanation, the available cultural evidence, and what actually ran. Missing evidence and fallback use remain visible, so you can inspect the basis for the recommendation.
    An overview of the system. Actual execution and any fallback appear with your comparison results.
    

    04 / Measured performance
    Tested on real preferences.
    Reported with context.
    Our final comparison uses 100 held-out MovieLens users. Each method chooses between two films using the same eight visible ratings.
    This measures two-film preference prediction. It does not establish real-user satisfaction or open-ended recommendation quality.
    Evaluation scope and uncertainty
    The reported Qloo gain over Agent is 2 percentage points, with a paired 95% interval of −2 to +6 points (p = 0.625). The difference is not statistically significant. It is our own offline benchmark, not an official competition score.
